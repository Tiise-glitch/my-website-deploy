About Paws & Hearts

Our History

Paws & Hearts Animal Shelter began as a small community rescue initiative created to help vulnerable and abandoned animals across the local area.

Our Mission and Vision

We rescue, care for, and rehome animals in need while working to reduce the number of stray and neglected pets in our community.

Our Volunteer Team

  • Shelter Coordinator

    Oversees daily animal care, intake, and adoption support.

  • Veterinary Volunteers

    Provide checks, treatment support and healthy care planning for rescue animals.

  • Adoption Volunteers

    Help foster pets and match them with loving, stable homes.
