About Paws And Hearts

Our History

Paws & Hearts Animal Shelter is a hypothetical non-profit organisation, imagined as having originally been made by a small group of volunteers to rescue, rehabilitate stray and abandoned animals in the local community

Our Mission And Vision

To rescue and care for vulnerable animals, find them loving permanent homes, and reduce the number of stray animals.
